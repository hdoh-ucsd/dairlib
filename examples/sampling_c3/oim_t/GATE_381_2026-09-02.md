## Why the gating half is parked (two validated failure mechanisms)

Attempt 1 (`results/xarm6_gate381_exact8000_4a4e42`, kept as negative
evidence): the join consumed the instantaneous 2 ms point-pair sample.
Measured: instantaneous contact is intermittent during real continuous
pushes (163 of 219 unjoined transactions were `active=0` mid-push with
0.03-0.14 N on neighboring joined samples; ~24% inactive duty inside
episodes — the same physics that forces the simulator's own 0.05 s
episode-end debounce). A 3-transaction absence debounce killed a genuine
dwell at step 395; zero productive cycles; terminal hold from update 1,402.

Attempt 2 (`results/xarm6_gate381b_exact8000_983a16`): episode-level
receipt. The join then ran perfectly — **726 consecutive physically joined
dwell steps** — until the physical episode genuinely ended: the tip held at
a ±micron geometric gap transmitting ~zero force for >50 ms while the
object had already moved 16.3 mm. The dwell reset at 726 < 1000; the
response was declared incomplete; the machinery discarded a physically
productive push and parked for 6,400 updates.

## The finding

`successor_minimum_contact_steps = 1000` was calibrated under geometric
dwell counting, which credits physically force-free tail stretches. Under
any physically honest counting (both attempts), the bar is unreachable on
this task profile: the real push completes its object motion in ~700
physically active steps and the plan's tail transmits no force. Making the
physical receipt authoritative for dwell *accounting* therefore requires a
criterion-semantics decision that is out of scope for this loop:

- (a) keep the 1000-step bar and define it in controller-engagement units
  (geometric latch bounded by physical-episode liveness), or
- (b) define completeness in physically joined units and recalibrate the
  bar against measured joined-step distributions (e.g. the 726 measured
  here), or
- (c) replace step-count completeness with measured object-response
  completeness (progress thresholds already exist).

All three touch pre-registered semantics and need explicit authorization.
The phantom-dwell *interval* problem (7.12 s of claims with no contact)
remains measured and is now visible live via `phys_joined=0` stretches in
the transaction stream.
