## Defect

The exact-8,000 run (`results/xarm6_gates375_379_exact8000_final2_v8rOvB`,
analysis in `docs/experiments/xarm6_exact8000_after_update_4000.md`) stalled
after update 4,000 in a lift/traverse/rejected-lower/replan loop: 12
`posture_progress=REJECT` receipts at `current_tip_error_m=0.01`,
`target_tip_error_m=0.0130-0.0135`, 11 invalidated candidates over updates
4,190-5,618, and no object contact for roughly 107 seconds.

Root cause, reproduced offline at the measured joint state from
`xarm_control.csv` t=131.110 s (replayed `target_tip_error` 0.013328 vs the
logged 0.0132073):

1. `SolveVerticalContactPostureStep` and `SolveCollisionAwarePostureStep`
   solved full nonlinear IK to each local Cartesian sub-step and then clamped
   each joint independently to `velocity_limits * planning_time_step`.
   Componentwise clamping distorts the solved joint ratio: a coordinated
   vertical descent needs roughly joint5:joint3 = 2:1, and the clamp forces
   1:1 (in-run receipt: `qdot_command` saturating at +0.5000/-0.5000 on both
   joints; measured joint travel ratio 0.88). The under-applied wrist share
   tilts the capsule off the vertical band (2.79 -> 6.05 deg during the
   failing descent) and, once the state has drifted, the clamped step moves
   the tip *away* from its command (commanded xy (+0.0063, +0.0065),
   executed (-0.0028, -0.0029)), which the unchanged progressive gate then
   correctly refuses.
2. The vertical-contact IK carried only the 0.05 rad axis *band constraint*.
   The source OIM controller keeps the stick vertical with a large tilt
   *cost* (`w_tilt: 80.0`, cost `w_tilt (1 - cos psi)`, in
   `oim/configs/robots/xarm6.yaml` and `xarm6_real.yaml`, never faded); the
   port had dropped the objective, so the nearest-q IK legally hugged the
   band edge. The componentwise distortion had been masking this: it
   over-verticalized some geometries by accident (0.75 deg contact arrival in
   the gate-tier baseline) and under-verticalized others (the exact-8000
   deadlock geometry).

The candidate previews step waypoints with a perfect executor, under which
even the componentwise clamp converges, so `ik_reached=1` kept admitting
candidates whose live lowering deadlocked.

## Change

No solver setting, cost matrix, contact parameter, seed, task tolerance,
safety gate, or acceptance threshold was changed.

1. `LimitXarmFullSamplingC3JointStepPreservingDirection` (new library helper
   with input validation): scales the whole IK joint step so the
   most-saturated joint runs at its velocity window; identity whenever the
   step is within limits. Replaces the componentwise clamp at both call
   sites (`SolveVerticalContactPostureStep`,
   `SolveCollisionAwarePostureStep`).
2. `SolveVerticalContactPostureStep` restores the source stick-tilt
   objective: `AddAngleBetweenVectorsCost(..., 80.0)` (exact source
   `w_tilt`), alongside the unchanged band constraint.
