## Gate 382: OSC trajectory sources must not extrapolate past their message

Root cause (measured in `xarm6_gate381diag_exact8000_6cbd6f`): the OSC's
`SafeTaskTrajectorySource` and `SafeForceTrajectorySource` built raw
`FirstOrderHold`s from message knots; Drake trajectories extrapolate the
closest segment beyond end time, so once the planner went silent after a
release, the desired position ramped away at release velocity without bound.
Measured runaway: tip driven from the release point to z = 1.14 m and
x = -0.82 m across ~15 s, joint 3 into its -3.90 rad limit; the protective
quiescence gate then refused a handoff while the arm flailed. In the
baseline this same mechanism shaped every run's ending (2a9877's terminal
hold tip sat at z = 0.88 m).

Fix: `FirstOrderHoldWithTerminalHold` — one appended zero-slope terminal
knot, mirroring the existing collision-aware posture-trajectory hold —
applied to both sources (`xarm6_osc_controller.cc`: helper after
`FindTrajectory`; both raw `FirstOrderHold` call sites replaced).

Validation (`xarm6_gate382_oscterm_exact8000_5d51ce`): post-release tip
bounded (max z 0.186 m in the release window), no joint-limit dive, 7
handoffs PASS, 7 cycles (2 PASS), 0 posture REJECTs, terminal FAIL
0.738/2.794.

## Gate 383: quiescence waits must charge the budget per execution period

Root cause: `wait_for_tip_quiescence` was the only one of seven
`++full_execution_updates` loops that incremented per 2 ms state message
instead of per 20 ms execution period. Every quiescence wait was billed
10x: the terminal settle in both the baseline and gate-382 runs was ~6.9 s
of sim time billed as 3,448-4,139 updates — 43-52% of the entire 8,000
budget. The 3-consecutive-settled-samples test also sampled a 6 ms window
instead of the intended planning interval.

Fix: period pacing (the `next_utime` pattern of the other six loops) plus
one phase-tagged refresh at entry.

Validation (`xarm6_gate383_quiescepace_exact8000_7832f9`): settle episodes
collapsed from `[3x7, 4139]` to `[1x8]`, 15 quiescence PASS / 0 FAIL, the
run stayed in live manipulation through update 8,000
(`productive_handoff_preserved=1`), 8 planning handoffs, 7 cycles with
**3 PASS** (most to date), terminal FAIL 0.732/2.804 with 0.337 rad yaw
progress. Five `posture_step=REJECT` clearance events appeared and were
absorbed by the existing recovery machinery (queued for review).

## Standing conclusions

- No solver setting, cost, contact parameter, seed, tolerance, or
  acceptance threshold changed in either gate.
- With the tail eliminated, per-run throughput is bounded by real
  acquisition motion: each reposition route (mandatory high-neutral
  routing at 10 mm sub-steps and 0.18 m/s) costs ~700-1,000 updates per
  cycle. That routing cost is now the top throughput item, alongside the
  Gate-381 dwell-semantics decision already parked for authorization.
