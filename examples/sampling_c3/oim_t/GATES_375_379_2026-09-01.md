## Result

These gates join simulator-measured point-pair contact to the controller's
timestamped selected-face transaction. They change diagnostics only: no
solver, controller, cost, horizon, seed, contact parameter, or terminal
tolerance was changed.

| Gate | Acceptance evidence | Result |
| ---: | --- | :---: |
| 375 | Emit timestamped controller receipts containing phase, sample identity, selected boundary point and normal in world coordinates, predicted-prefix ownership, geometric-contact ownership, and dwell ownership. | PASS |
| 376 | Normalize Drake's point-pair force to force on the manipulated object and join each 2 ms raw contact observation to the nearest 20 ms controller transaction. | PASS |
| 377 | Verify that the measured contact point belongs to the selected sample neighborhood and that measured force has inward polarity against the selected outward normal. | PASS |
| 378 | Keep predicted-prefix, geometric-contact, controller-dwell, and physically joined dwell ownership as separate facts. A failed join never deletes the raw contact receipt. | PASS |
| 379 | Render accepted joins green, explicit join rejections red, and unjoined raw tip candidates yellow; write a machine-readable join summary and verify the unchanged closed loop. | PASS |

For selected object-boundary point `b_W`, outward normal `n_W`, measured
contact point `p_C`, and Drake force on the object `f_O`, the join computes

```text
e_face = ||p_C,xy - b_W||
f_normal = -n_W' f_O,xy

face_identity   := e_face <= 2 r_pusher + contact_activation_tolerance
normal_polarity := f_normal > 0

accepted_contact :=
    raw_class == tip_side_candidate
    and face_identity
    and normal_polarity
    and predicted_prefix_owner
    and geometric_contact_owner.

dwell_owned_contact := accepted_contact and controller_dwell_owner.
```

No force deadband was introduced. Small negative or positive normal components
remain visible as measured, rather than being reclassified by an unapproved
tolerance.

receipts and 4,964 raw Drake contact observations across three physical

| Physically accepted dwell observations | 4,319 |
| Rejected controller dwell claims | 631 |

The most important new receipt is temporal: physical episode 1 ended at
`35.542 s` and episode 2 did not begin until `42.688 s`, but the controller
continued to claim dwell ownership for 357 consecutive 20 ms transaction
receipts from `35.554–42.674 s`. The previous geometric latch therefore
credited about 7.12 seconds of dwell while Drake reported no xArm/T contact.
This explains the visually apparent phantom-contact interval and is now
measured rather than inferred from video.

The wrong-polarity values are predominantly small normal components; their
observed range is approximately `-1.35e-4` to `-2.55e-9 N`. The accepted
normal component range starts at approximately `9.03e-9 N` and reaches
`7.35 N`. This gate intentionally reports that sign-sensitive distribution
without adding a force threshold.

The terminal result remains `open_table_success=FAIL`: translation error is
`0.784027 m` and wrapped-yaw error is `3.08103 rad` versus unchanged `0.05 m`
and `0.10 rad` tolerances. The planner exits with the existing
`measured_cycle_budget_deferred_before_terminal` receipt at update 1,909 of
2,000.

## Next gate

Gate 380 must make the live physical-contact receipt authoritative for dwell
accounting. The simulator should publish timestamped contact point,
force-on-object, and raw-active state on a dedicated channel; the controller
should join that fresh receipt to its selected-face transaction before
incrementing dwell. Missing, stale, wrong-face, wrong-polarity, or physically
absent contact must not accrue dwell, while the existing geometric latch may
remain as a prediction/command-continuity diagnostic.
