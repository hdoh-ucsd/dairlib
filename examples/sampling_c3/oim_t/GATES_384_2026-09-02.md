## Gate 384: reserve measured acquisition overhead (open checklist item)

`EvaluateFullSamplingC3CycleBudget` gains a `minimum_acquisition_updates`
floor (default 0) and the measured variant accepts
`measured_acquisition_updates`; the worst measured receipt floors the
live-IK estimate the same way release/recovery and contact-phase receipts
already reserve their worst measured durations. The controller records each
progress cycle's acquisition span (progress_lift start to contact reached,
`acquisition_receipt=PASS` log) and feeds the vector at admission. Unit test
`MeasuredAcquisitionReceiptsFloorTheBudgetEstimate` (76 native tests green).

## Gate 384b: sub-clearance release retry

The first validation run (`xarm6_gate384_acqreserve_exact8000_1c3299`,
kept as the failure receipt) deferred at update 1,890: the dynamic release
stalled 60 um short of the 5 mm clearance. Mechanism: a freshly commanded
padded sub-clearance step was spuriously "entry-reached" (residual tip
speed widens the settle prediction past the step), published nothing, and
the zero-update guard abandoned the release — an edge previously masked by
the gate-382 extrapolation runaway drifting the tip past clearance. Fix:
the guard now waits one budget-charged execution period and retries from
the fresh measured state (`corrective_release_progress=RETRY`), so it can
neither spin free nor abandon microns short.

## Measured end-of-run policy frontier (parked for authorization)

Live manipulation ended at update 4,108 with 49% of budget in terminal
hold: the 8th cycle was an admitted rotation-trade candidate
(predicted +0.357 rad yaw for -24 mm translation; measured +0.069 rad)
that left lateral drift at 5.94 mm, and every one of 128 executable
recovery candidates was rejected by the recovery admission gate (each
reduces lateral 2.5 mm at a 3.4 mm translation debt above the bound). The
recovery admission is stricter than the progress admission that created
the debt; reconciling them, and the ~5x yaw over-prediction, are
pre-registered-semantics decisions queued for authorization alongside the
Gate-381 dwell-units decision.
